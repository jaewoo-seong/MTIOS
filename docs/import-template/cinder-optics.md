# Cinder Optics

## Summary
Cinder Optics builds modular robotic arms and grippers for small-batch
manufacturing. They qualify because they are currently fundraising and
manufacture physical hardware in-house.

## Profile
- Founded: 2023
- Location: Kyoto, Japan
- Team size: 18
- Stage: Live campaign

## Findings
- Raised $50,000 against a $40,000 goal as of 2026-03-14.
- Manufactures in-house rather than outsourcing assembly.
- Sells primarily to contract manufacturers, not consumers.

## Fit
Their in-house assembly line is the kind of operation our services target,
and the current raise means budget is being allocated now.
